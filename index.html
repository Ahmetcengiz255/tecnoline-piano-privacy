Tecnoline Piano Studio

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 13, 2026

Tecnoline Piano Studio is an offline piano learning and music creation application developed by Tecnoline.

1. Personal Information

The app does not require account registration or personal information to use its features.

2. Local Data Storage

Lesson progress, performance results and saved compositions are stored locally on the user's device. The app does not operate a server to collect this data.

Some app data may be included in Android system backups, depending on device settings and backup rules.

3. Internet and Advertising

The app is designed to function offline and does not include third-party advertising or analytics services.

4. File Export

Users can export musical compositions as MIDI or PDF files to locations they select on their devices.

5. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Security

Application data is stored locally. Users are encouraged to protect their devices and exported files.

7. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change.
